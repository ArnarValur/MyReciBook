## Full description (4000 max)

Rescue the recipes buried in your camera roll.

MyReciBook reads screenshots, links and handwritten cards — and files them
into a cookbook you actually own. On your phone, like a card in a box.
No subscription. No account. Ever.

HOW A RESCUE WORKS
1. Share a screenshot, paste a link, or snap grandma's card with the camera.
2. The AI turns it into a proper recipe — ingredients, steps and timings —
   and shows you the result to review before it's saved.
3. File it in your book. It lives on your phone; export a PDF or document
   whenever you like.

Grandma's handwriting welcome — we read cursive.

WHAT'S FILED IN THE BOX
• Cook mode — one step at a time in big, kitchen-proof type. Tap zones
  sized for floury thumbs, timers built in.
• Grocery list — "9 items · from 3 planned recipes" — merged, deduplicated,
  and it asks before combining 2 lemons with 4.
• Pantry — know what's on the shelf. Categories fold away, recents float
  up, and the grocery list checks against it.
• Food diary — log meals straight from your recipes and pantry, no double
  typing. Trends show your week without the guilt trip.
• Barcode scanner — point it at the package. Open Food Facts fills in the
  product and its nutrition — most countries covered.
• Nutrition tracking — energy, macros and micros per day, week and year,
  with coverage you can actually see.

YOURS, NOT RENTED
Your recipes stay on your phone — plain files in a folder you choose.
Google Drive and Dropbox are available for backup, and any recipe exports
as a PDF or document. If MyReciBook vanished tomorrow, your recipes
wouldn't.

THE PRICE
Pay once. Like a good knife.
• Everything included — cookbook, cook mode, grocery, pantry, diary
• 1,200 AI recipe rescues — included in the price
• Typing recipes in yourself — always unlimited
• Ran out? Top up the AI — still no subscription

No subscription. No account. Ever.
