What it does

Three promises, kept in writing.

photo_library

Screenshots first

Instagram, TikTok, your mum on WhatsApp. Pick one screenshot or a whole pile — one recipe across five shots gets stitched, five different saves become five recipes. Nothing is saved silently: you review, you confirm, done. Cookbook pages and handwritten cards welcome, by camera.

folder

Your files, not our cloud

One plain, readable file per recipe, in a folder you pick, on your phone. Back up to your own Google Drive or Dropbox. Delete the app and every recipe is still there. No account. No login. No tracking. No ads. Ever.

Know your food

Your pantry knows its numbers.

Scan a barcode off the shelf and the pantry fills itself — name, brand, pack size and the full nutrient list per 100g, vitamins included when the database knows them. Link "250 ml milk" to the actual milk you scanned, and recipes show per-serving numbers computed from your own food. Build a new recipe straight out of the pantry. Log what you actually ate, day by day. All of it is AI-free — the whole nutrition kit lives on the never-capped side, forever.

Ownership

Delete the app. Keep every recipe.

The folder is the app. Every recipe is one JSON file you can open in any text editor, on any machine, forever. Backup is a one-way mirror to your own Drive or Dropbox — restore never overwrites. The settings screen has three blocks, because there is no telemetry to toggle. And there are no ads, no popups, no tricks — not now, not ever. That's not a tier; it's the whole app.
