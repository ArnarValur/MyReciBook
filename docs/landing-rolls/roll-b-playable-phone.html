What you just did, for real

Three promises, kept in writing.

photo_library

Screenshots first

Instagram, TikTok, your mum on WhatsApp. One screenshot or a whole pile — one recipe across five shots gets stitched, five saves become five recipes. You review, you confirm, done. Handwritten cards welcome, by camera.

folder

Your files, not our cloud

One plain, readable file per recipe, in a folder you pick, on your phone. Back up to your own Google Drive or Dropbox. Delete the app and every recipe is still there. No account. No login. No tracking. No ads. Ever.

paid

Pay once

You buy MyReciBook like you'd buy a good knife: once. AI rescues have a fair-use cap stated in writing — and typing recipes in yourself is always free and unlimited.

Also in the box

The quiet workhorses.

call_merge The grocery list that actually merges

Add two recipes and "lemon" + "lemons" become one row — 6 lemons, from 2 recipes — and only after asking you. Move an item to your own aisle once and it lands there every time after. Salt, pepper and oil stay quiet unless you tap them.

barcode_scanner Your pantry knows its numbers

Scan a barcode and the pantry fills itself — brand, pack size, full nutrients per 100g. Recipes show per-serving numbers computed from your own food. Log what you actually ate, day by day. The whole nutrition kit is AI-free and lives on the never-capped side, forever.

bolt Plus: cook mode that keeps the screen awake and finds timers inside steps · as-written / metric / US toggle · favorites and tags that live in your files · search across everything.
