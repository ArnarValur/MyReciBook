The house card — white fill, hairline border, radius 12–16, soft blue-tinted shadow. Everything card-like in MyReciBook is one of these.

```jsx
<TokenCard padding={14}>…rows…</TokenCard>
<TokenCard radius={16} padding={16} selected>…price card…</TokenCard>
```

`selected` = 1.5px primary border + Moody Blue glow, the "this one" treatment (selected storage option, merge prompt, paywall price). Ingredient/step/grocery lists sit inside one card with hairline separators between rows (`padding="2px 14px"`).
