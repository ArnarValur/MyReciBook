The pitch
"Rescue the recipes buried in your camera roll." Rescue is the house verb — never "scan" or "digitize".
Empty state
"Your book is empty (for now)" — playful, never guilt. Sells the next action, not the lack.
Failure, calm
"That one kept its secrets." Then three reassurances: gallery untouched, nothing deleted, cap unspent.
The promise register
"Pay once. Cook forever." · "Because your recipe box shouldn't have a landlord." · "If MyReciBook vanished tomorrow, your recipes wouldn't."
