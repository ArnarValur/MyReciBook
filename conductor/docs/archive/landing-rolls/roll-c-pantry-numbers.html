Oh — and the other half of the app

It rescues the recipes buried in your camera roll.

photo_library

Screenshots become recipes. Instagram, TikTok, your mum on WhatsApp. Pick one screenshot or a pile; you review, you confirm, done. AI rescues carry a fair-use cap stated in writing — typing recipes in yourself is always free and unlimited.

folder

Your files, not our cloud. One plain, readable file per recipe, in a folder you pick. Back up to your own Drive or Dropbox. Delete the app and every recipe — and your food log — is still there. No account. No login. No tracking. No ads. Ever.

call_merge

A grocery list that actually merges. "Lemon" + "lemons" become one row — 6 lemons, from 2 recipes — and only after asking you. Your aisles, remembered. Staples stay quiet.
