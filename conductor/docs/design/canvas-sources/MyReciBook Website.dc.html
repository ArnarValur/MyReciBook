How it works

Three steps between the pile and the plate.

photo_library
1 · Point it at the pile

Share a screenshot, paste a link, or snap grandma's card with the camera — handwriting welcome. You pick what we see; we never scan your gallery.

auto_awesome
2 · We read it twice

The AI lifts ingredients, steps and timings into a clean recipe. Anywhere it guesses, it flags and asks — no silent automation, ever.

menu_book
3 · It lands in your book

A plain file on your phone, in a format you can read. The original screenshot stays one tap behind the cover.

check_circleNot a recipe? We skip it and say so — no junk lands in your book.
